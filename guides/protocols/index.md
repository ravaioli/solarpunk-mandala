# Protocols: The Living Architecture of Regeneration

The Solarpunk Mandala is not a theoretical construct—it is a living geometry of action. Protocols transform the Mandala's elegant framework into tangible, day-to-day practice. Each protocol is a field-tested method for navigating specific dimensions of community regeneration, with Embodied Foundations as their non-negotiable core.

## Core Protocol Sequence

The protocols form an integrated ecosystem, not a linear checklist:

**Protocol 0: Embodied Foundations Audit**  
*The Ground of All Action*  
Before any transformation can occur, basic human needs must be met. This protocol ensures communities have the physical and emotional stability required for meaningful systemic change. Functions as the operating system upon which all other protocols run.

**Protocol 1: Settlement Health Assessment**  
*Diagnosing Contraction and Regenerative Force*  
This protocol maps the current state of community health across the Four Axes of Transformation (Soteriological, Axiological, Relational, Temporal). It identifies where intervention is most needed, functioning as the diagnostic engine that guides strategic action.

**Protocol 2: Project Design Integration**  
*From Diagnosis to Wholeness*  
This protocol translates insights from Protocol 1 into concrete, multi-dimensional projects. It ensures holistic design that simultaneously heals contraction, integrates across the four axes, and catalyzes dimensional unfolding within the community.

**Protocol 3: Crisis Response Framework**  
*Navigating Emergencies While Protecting the Regenerative Core*  
When emergencies arise—natural disasters, economic shocks, health crises—the regenerative core must be protected. This protocol provides a phased structure (Triage→Assessment→Integration) to ensure crisis response strengthens rather than undermines long-term community resilience.

**Protocol 4: Mnemonic Integration & Memory Work**  
*Healing the Patterns of the Past*  
This protocol addresses the living architecture of identity and culture by diagnosing and transforming dysfunctional memory states (fragmentation, rigidity, amnesia). It heals narrative blockages to strengthen the Soteriological (self-meaning) and Temporal (time wisdom) axes.

**Protocol 5: Dinacharya & Daily Rhythms**  
*Harmonizing with Regenerative Intelligence*  
This protocol translates Mandala principles into the rhythmic fabric of daily life. It provides a framework for designing personal and communal routines that consciously align with natural and cosmic cycles, creating a stable container for all other work.

**Protocol 6: Local Food System Audit**  
*Mapping Bioregional Nourishment*  
This specialized audit tool diagnoses a community's food ecology through the integrated lenses of Solarpunk ethics and Ayurvedic wisdom. It generates a "Food Mandala Map" to reveal leverage points for enhancing food sovereignty, personal health, and systemic resilience.

## Protocol Selection Guide

| When to Activate | Primary Protocol | Foundation Requirements & Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Starting any community work**, after crisis, burnout patterns | **Protocol 0** | N/A (this protocol assesses the foundations). |
| **Beginning transformation work**, after major events, strategic planning | **Protocol 1** | All foundations ≥2.5. |
| **Designing new community initiatives** to address diagnosed needs | **Protocol 2** | Varies by focus; typically requires all foundations ≥3.0. |
| **Natural disasters, economic shocks, health emergencies** | **Protocol 3** | Focus on stabilizing any foundation below emergency threshold (1.5). |
| **Narrative blockages**, historical stuckness, cultural amnesia | **Protocol 4** | **Critical:** Cleansing & Restoration ≥3.0. Requires a strong container. |
| **Establishing/reforming daily culture**, recovering from chaos, seasonal shifts | **Protocol 5** | Restoration ≥3.5 for communal practice. Focus on Core Rhythmic Anchors. |
| **Assessing food sovereignty**, aligning diet with place, seasonal eating shift | **Protocol 6** | Nourishment ≥3.0. Ideal at solstice/equinox or when relocating. |

## Key Integration Principles

**Embodied Foundations First:** No protocol can proceed without stable foundations. If foundations score below threshold requirements, all other work pauses until foundations are restored via Protocol 0 or Protocol 3.

**Non-Linear, Recursive Flow:** While protocols follow a logical sequence (0→1→2, etc.), communities may move between them based on needs. Protocol 3 can interrupt any sequence during a crisis. Protocols 4, 5, and 6 are often activated cyclically (seasonally) or in response to specific triggers identified in Protocol 1.

**Pathway Activation:** Each protocol activates specific Mandala Pathways based on its focus:
*   **Protocol 0** primarily activates **Healing (Cura)**
*   **Protocol 1** primarily activates **Awakening (Conscientização)**
*   **Protocol 2** primarily activates **Making (Capacitação)** and **Liberation (Liberação)**
*   **Protocol 3** integrates all pathways based on crisis type
*   **Protocol 4** deeply engages **Healing (Cura)** and **Liberation (Liberação)**
*   **Protocol 5** embodies the **Path of Integration**, weaving all pathways into daily practice
*   **Protocol 6** applies **Awakening (Conscientização)** and **Making (Capacitação)** to a specific subsystem

**Boundary Medicine:** All protocols include boundary medicine practices that heal dissociation between:
*   Individual and collective
*   Spiritual and practical
*   Past and future
*   Human and ecological
*   Memory and present action (P4)
*   Daily routine and cosmic cycles (P5)
*   Consumer and bioregional participant (P6)

**Living Documentation:** Every protocol implementation contributes to the community's Living Archive and Rhizomatic Network, creating a wisdom repository that evolves with practice.
