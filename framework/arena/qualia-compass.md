*Bridging Felt Experience and Systemic Health*

## 📋 **Introduction**
This worksheet is the **diagnostic heart** of the Solarpunk Mandala's epistemic architecture. It translates the geometric and experiential logic of the Tesseract into a practical tool for sensing, diagnosing, and regenerating community health across scales.

### How This Worksheet Leverages the Mandala's Epistemology

The Mandala is built on an **epistemic tesseract**—a 4D model for holding multiple perspectives simultaneously without reductionism. This worksheet operationalizes that geometry through two parallel frameworks:

1. **The Experiential Topography** (Qualia of Civilizational Paradigms)  
   Maps the eight core facets of lived experience, from degenerative (Necrocene) to regenerative (Symbiotic) realities.

2. **The Capital Framework** (20 Forms of Regenerative Capital)  
   Measures the tangible stocks of value across five domains (ECOLOGY, ECONOMICS, POLITICS, CULTURE, SPIRITUALITY).

### The Parallel Analysis: A Fourfold Correspondence

Both frameworks emerge from the same fourfold logic of the Tesseract, creating a precise mapping between **felt experience** and **systemic health**:

| Tesseract Dimension | Experiential Facets (Qualia) | Capital Types (in Each Domain) | Diagnostic Insight |
|---------------------|------------------------------|--------------------------------|-------------------|
| **Structure/Container** | Facets of form & stability (e.g., *Scarcity → Abundance*) | Structure Capitals (Regenerative, Productive, Aesthetic) | When structures feel scarce, check material foundations |
| **Process/Exchange** | Facets of flow & relationship (e.g., *Domination → Collaboration*) | Process Capitals (Adaptive, Reciprocity, Ritual) | When power feels dominating, examine relational exchanges |
| **Pattern/Information** | Facets of meaning & narrative (e.g., *Alienation → Symbiosis*) | Pattern Capitals (Biophilic, Knowledge, Narrative) | When connection feels broken, assess stories and codes |
| **Consciousness/Quality** | Facets of awareness & state (e.g., *Numbness → Aliveness*) | Consciousness Capitals (Stewardship, Convivial, Interbeing) | When life feels numb, cultivate presence and reverence |

### Why This Integration Matters

Traditional metrics measure **what we have** (capital stocks). This worksheet measures **how we experience what we have** (qualia). By correlating the two, we:

- **Diagnose root causes** of systemic issues at the level of consciousness
- **Design interventions** that address both felt reality and measurable outcomes
- **Track regeneration** in human terms, not just abstract metrics
- **Validate the Mandala's epistemology** through lived experience

**Use this worksheet during:**
- Seasonal community check-ins
- Conflict resolution processes
- Visioning and planning sessions
- After significant events or transitions
- When capital metrics don't match community wellbeing

## 🛠 **Part 3: Protocol Prescriptions**
*For your three lowest-scoring facets, here are suggested regeneration protocols.*

```yaml
Alienation → Symbiosis:
  - Protocol: "Place-Based Story Circles"
  - Description: Monthly gatherings where community members share personal and ancestral stories of connection to the land and each other.
  - Target Capitals: Identity, Relational
  - Expected Timeframe: 3–6 months for noticeable shift

Scarcity → Abundance:
  - Protocol: "Mutual Credit & Time Banking"
  - Description: Implement a local credit system where services and goods are exchanged without conventional currency.
  - Target Capitals: Productive, Reciprocity
  - Expected Timeframe: Immediate relief, 1 year for cultural integration

Domination → Collaboration:
  - Protocol: "Consent-Based Decision Making"
  - Description: Replace majority-rule voting with consent processes where decisions proceed unless there are reasoned objections.
  - Target Capitals: Civic, Wisdom
  - Expected Timeframe: 2–4 months for proficiency

Anxiety → Courageous Trust:
  - Protocol: "Collective Somatic Practices"
  - Description: Weekly community breathwork, grounding, or trauma-informed yoga sessions.
  - Target Capitals: Presence, Stewardship
  - Expected Timeframe: 1 month for initial calm, 6 months for resilience

Exploitation → Reverence:
  - Protocol: "Land Guardian Program"
  - Description: Assign stewardship roles for local ecosystems with regular monitoring and ceremony.
  - Target Capitals: Stewardship, Biophilic
  - Expected Timeframe: Ongoing, with seasonal evaluations

Numbness → Aliveness:
  - Protocol: "Beauty & Ritual Interventions"
  - Description: Install public art, hold seasonal festivals, and create daily micro-rituals.
  - Target Capitals: Aesthetic, Ritual
  - Expected Timeframe: Immediate sensory uplift

Linear Urgency → Cyclical Patience:
  - Protocol: "7th-Generation Planning"
  - Description: Require all major projects to include long-term impact assessments and legacy clauses.
  - Target Capitals: Wisdom, Ritual
  - Expected Timeframe: 1 year for cultural shift

Reductionism → Holism:
  - Protocol: "Cross-Domain Integration Labs"
  - Description: Quarterly workshops where different domain stewards (Ecology, Economics, etc.) design projects together.
  - Target Capitals: Knowledge, Interbeing
  - Expected Timeframe: 6–12 months for integrated thinking
```

## 🧩 **Part 5: Advanced Diagnostics**
*For communities ready to deepen their analysis.*

### **Pattern Recognition**
- Are low scores clustered in certain **domains** (e.g., all SPIRITUALITY-related facets)?
- Are there **polarizations** (some rate a facet 1, others rate it 10)?
- Do scores shift seasonally or with specific events?

### **Intervention Layers**
For each low-scoring facet, consider interventions at:
1. **Individual**: Personal practices, therapy, skill-building
2. **Relational**: Dialogue formats, conflict transformation, trust-building
3. **Systemic**: Policy changes, structural redesign, resource reallocation
4. **Cultural**: Narrative work, art, ritual, education

### **Falsification Check**
If capital scores improve but qualia scores don’t:
- Are we measuring the right things?
- Is there perceptual resistance or trauma?
- Are improvements not yet felt at the human scale?
